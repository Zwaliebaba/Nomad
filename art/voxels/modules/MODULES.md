# Station screenshot modules

The 24 PascalCase `.vox` files reconstruct the distinct module types in the owner's
2026-11-07 isometric station screenshot. Repeated structures share a file; the
three tower silhouettes, two solar-array sizes, three platform sizes and four
tube junctions have separate files. Ships, asteroids and the starfield are not
base modules and are excluded.

These are visual reference assets. Hidden walls, undersides and interior details
are inferred, and descriptive names do not assign new gameplay rules. Existing
module files are preserved. The assets are registered in `set.json`, and their
game-readable JSON is generated by `nomad-vox` (D-289). They are not automatically
placed into the current station scene or assigned to gameplay slots.

## Editing and coordinates

Open any file in MagicaVoxel 0.99.6 or later. Files use format 200, a named scene
object and a shared 22-colour palette. The complete RGBA and material tables are
identical across all 24 files, including unused slots. The original 21 RGB colours
are preserved without quantisation. `ModulePalette.json` documents the
1-based file indices and material parameters:

| Index | Role | MagicaVoxel material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | Cyan lights | Emit |
| 10 | White emitters | Emit |
| 11 | Red beacons | Emit |
| 12 | Amber energy | Emit |
| 16 | Blue glazing | Glass |
| 22 | Original blue frame colour (same RGB as 16) | Diffuse |
| Remaining used indices | Hull, structure, markings, solar cells and planting | Diffuse |

The biodome and twin greenhouses have Glass enclosure voxels between their
original frame ribs. Existing frames, planting, lamps and original surface
colours remain intact; the new glazing uses a distinct material from the frame,
even where their RGB is the same. Glass has low roughness and an IOR parameter
of 0.45 (the MagicaVoxel normalised parameter, corresponding to about 1.45).
Emission is defined by MATL chunks, not just bright-looking palette colours.

**Current game limitation:** `nomad-vox` preserves RGB and Emit only. The generated
game catalog renders Glass as an opaque colour, and exporting that catalog back
to `.vox` loses Glass and the shared editing palette. Keep these `.vox` files as
the sources of truth; do not use a catalog export to refresh their materials.
Transparent rendering in the client requires a separate rendering change.

`+z` is up; footprints are centred about x/y, with the original slot datum at z=0.
This datum is an assembly reference, not a ground-contact plane. Modules have
finished lower hulls extending below it, with structural ribs, flush equipment
panels and recessed attitude-control nozzles. No active exhaust is modelled.
Solar arrays use a suspended central spine and braced wing pivots instead of
ground-like end piers. Connectors are enclosed circular tubes, with reinforced
coupling collars, dark inner liners, structural ribs and cyan service lights.
Each tube has a clear bore through every junction; there are no internal caps.
The elbow follows a swept quarter-circle. All mouths share the same cross-section
(outer collar radius 6.2 voxels, clear bore radius 3.2 voxels), with the tube axis
at z=9. Port centres remain 16 voxels from the origin on their original axes.
Model IDs use lowercase snake_case without an asset prefix; `set.json` maps
each ID to its PascalCase source filename using `file`. The connectors keep
their geometric connection positions; their round shells and
collars are taller and wider than the previous open bridges. The ring dock retains
its central aperture and has annular underside plating.
The hangar opens towards `+x`. Dimensions follow the reference's visual proportions
rather than the existing gameplay slot sizes. `ModuleInventory.json` records
each file's bounds, voxel count, Glass count and individual space-module review.
The material/underside pass retained original voxel coordinates and RGB values
except 708 solar support voxels below z=7, replaced with suspension hardware.
The later tube redesign replaces the four connector geometries; the other
modules retain their upper silhouettes. Holographic ring marks are luminous voxels
by design. These are visual art changes, not simulation or propulsion rules.

After editing, run from the repository root:

```bash
cargo run --locked -p nomad-vox -- import
cargo run --locked -p nomad-vox -- import --check
cargo test --locked -p nomad-vox
python3 tools/vox/audit_modules.py
```

Keep the `.vox` sources and regenerated JSON together. The existing reference
modules do not replace the original gameplay models. The Python audit checks
every file's complete palette, material table, geometry encoding, used material
slots and inventory counts; it catches material changes that the RGB/Emit-only
game catalog cannot detect. For tubes it also checks matching port profiles,
connected interior bores and shell leaks on the saved voxel geometry.
